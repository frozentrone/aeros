ABOUT THE MODEL

Particle Charge Calculator 2D

Estimates particle charge from horizontal landing position. Numerical mode integrates gravity, electric force and drag. Analytical and vacuum modes use a uniform electric-field approximation.

Use the numerical results as model estimates and validate assumptions, coordinate conventions and units against the original experimental method.
